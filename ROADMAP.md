# Roadmap

Where ANIMA stands and what comes next. The roadmap is a plan, not a promise: it changes via pull request as the work progresses. Each item links to an issue where progress is tracked.

*As of 2026-11-11.*

## Done

- **v0.1.0** – first scaffold of the repository
- **v0.2.0** – restructured around the triad of client, practitioner and AI system; scope, methodology, data format, validation, website ([#10](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/10))

## Now: finding the sources

- **Search protocol and candidate register** – documented search following scoping-review practice ([#18](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/pull/18))
- **Search, pillar by pillar** – law, then guidance, then professional standards; screening of all candidates
- **First source files** – starting with EU law (AI Act, GDPR, MDR), each requirement mapped onto the triad

## Next: making ANIMA usable

| Item | What | Depends on | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glossary** | all terms of ANIMA in one place, for readers from psychology, law and technology | – can grow now | [#19](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/19) |
| **FAQ** | answers for psychologists, people who use AI on their own, developers and institutions | answers to content questions need included sources | [#20](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/20) |
| **Practice guides** | one guide per typical situation, drawn from the navigator | included sources for the situation | [#21](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/21) |
| **Bilingual website** | English and German; English stays authoritative, translations reviewed by a native speaker | German terms for key concepts | [#23](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/23) |

## Later

- **Evidence pillar** – own search protocol for scientific literature; certainty rated following GRADE
- **Reviewers** – a second person for screening and review, so that content can move from `draft` to `reviewed`
- **Further countries** – national law and professional bodies beyond Austria, with contributors who can check them
- **Further EU languages** – stage 2: French, Spanish, Italian; stage 3: Polish, Dutch, Portuguese; each only with a native-speaking reviewer ([#23](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/23))

## Towards version 1.0

A proposal for when ANIMA is ready for version 1.0:

- the core sources for the EU and Austria are included and **reviewed** by a second person;
- the glossary and the FAQ cover the included sources;
- at least three practice guides are available;
- the protocol for the evidence pillar is defined;
- at least one reviewer besides the maintainer has been appointed (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#build-up-phase-before-v10)).
