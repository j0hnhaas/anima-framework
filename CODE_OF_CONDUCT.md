# Code of Conduct

ANIMA brings together people from psychology, psychotherapy, law, technology and people with lived experience of mental health conditions. We want a community in which everyone can contribute safely and with respect.

## Our standards

We expect everyone who takes part in ANIMA to:

- treat others with respect, regardless of profession, discipline, background, identity or experience;
- argue with sources and reasons, not against people;
- accept that disagreement is normal and productive in a scientific project;
- give and accept constructive feedback;
- be mindful that topics around mental health can be personal and sensitive.

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, discriminatory or degrading remarks;
- personal attacks or deliberate intimidation;
- publishing private information of others without their consent;
- sharing personal health information of third parties, including clients or patients, in any form;
- advertising products or services.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all ANIMA spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and events, and to anyone representing ANIMA in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) via their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's built-in *Report content* function. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on appropriate measures, depending on the severity: a private note, a public warning, a temporary or a permanent exclusion from the project. Decisions are documented internally.
