# Layer 3 – European Union

Binding EU law and official guidance relevant to AI in psychology and mental health.

> **Status:** to be researched. Application dates and amendments (e.g. through omnibus simplification packages) must be checked against [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu) before publishing.

## Core legal acts

| Act | Reference | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Act** | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 | Prohibited practices (Art. 5), high-risk classification (Art. 6, Annex I and III), transparency for chatbots and emotion recognition (Art. 50), AI literacy (Art. 4) |
| **GDPR** | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 | Health data as special category (Art. 9), automated decisions (Art. 22), data protection impact assessment (Art. 35) |
| **Medical Device Regulation (MDR)** | Regulation (EU) 2017/745 | Software as a medical device, classification rule 11 |
| **European Health Data Space (EHDS)** | Regulation (EU) 2025/327 | Electronic health records, secondary use of health data |
| **Product Liability Directive** | Directive (EU) 2024/2853 | Liability for defective software, including AI |
