# Pillar: guidance

Recommendations of international and public bodies, and official interpretations of the law by authorities. Bindingness: `official` (interpretation by an authority) or `recommended`.

## Candidate sources

Candidates for this pillar are listed in the **[candidate register](../../search/candidates.csv)** (filter by `pillar = guidance`) and screened following the [search protocol](../../docs/search-protocol.md). Check title, version and whether a newer edition exists before summarising.

**International references** – sources from outside the EU, e.g. from the United Kingdom or the United States – are included as good practice. They are not binding in the EU.

## How to summarise WHO documents

WHO publications are usually licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO**, which is not compatible with ANIMA's CC BY 4.0. Therefore:

- summarise in your own words,
- quote only short passages, clearly marked,
- always link to the original on [iris.who.int](https://iris.who.int).

Check the licence of every other guidance document before quoting from it.
