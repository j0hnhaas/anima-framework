## How to summarise WHO documents

WHO publications are usually licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO**, which is not compatible with ANIMA's CC BY 4.0. Therefore:

- summarise in your own words,
- quote only short passages, clearly marked,
- always link to the original on [iris.who.int](https://iris.who.int).

Check the licence of every other guidance document before quoting from it.
