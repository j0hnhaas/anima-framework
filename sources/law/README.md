# Pillar: law

Binding law relevant to psychology as a profession and to psychological effects of AI. Bindingness: `binding`.

## Candidate sources

Candidates for this pillar are listed in the **[candidate register](../../search/candidates.csv)** (filter by `pillar = law`) and screened following the [search protocol](../../docs/search-protocol.md). Check the current consolidated version and application dates before summarising – AI law in particular is changing quickly. Austrian law: always use the current version in the [RIS](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at) (Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes).

## Reuse of legal texts

- **EU law** may be reused with attribution (see the EUR-Lex legal notice).
- **Austrian laws** and official decrees are not protected by copyright (§ 7 UrhG) and may be quoted freely.
