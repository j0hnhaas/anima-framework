# Pillar: law

Binding law relevant to psychology as a profession and to psychological effects of AI. Bindingness: `binding`.

> **Status:** candidate sources, **to be verified**. Check the current consolidated version and application dates before summarising – AI law in particular is changing quickly.

## Candidate sources

### European Union (`eu`)

| Source | Reference | Why relevant |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Act** | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 | prohibited practices such as manipulation and exploitation of vulnerabilities; transparency for AI systems that interact with people; high-risk AI systems, incl. use in employment and education; AI literacy |
| **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 | health and other sensitive data; automated individual decisions; data protection impact assessment |
| **Medical Device Regulation (MDR)** | Regulation (EU) 2017/745 | software with a medical purpose, e.g. for diagnosis or treatment of mental disorders |
| **European Health Data Space (EHDS)** | Regulation (EU) 2025/327 | electronic health records, secondary use of health data |
| **Product Liability Directive** | Directive (EU) 2024/2853 | liability for defective software, including AI systems |

### Pan-European (`europe`)

| Source | Reference | Why relevant |
|---|---|---|
| **Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law** | CETS No. 225 (2024) | binding for states that ratify it; human dignity, autonomy, transparency. Ratification status to be checked. |

### Austria (`at`)

| Source | Why relevant |
|---|---|
| **Psychologengesetz 2013 (PlG)** | professional duties of clinical and health psychologists, e.g. confidentiality, information of clients, documentation, due diligence |
| **Datenschutzgesetz (DSG)** | national supplement to the GDPR |
| **Medizinproduktegesetz 2021 (MPG)** | national rules complementing the MDR |
| **Gesundheitstelematikgesetz 2012 (GTelG)** | electronic health data |
| **National implementation of the AI Act** | competent authorities and procedures – to be researched |

Always check the current version in the [RIS](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at) (Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes).

## Reuse of legal texts

- **EU law** may be reused with attribution (see the EUR-Lex legal notice).
- **Austrian laws** and official decrees are not protected by copyright (§ 7 UrhG) and may be quoted freely.
