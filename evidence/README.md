# Pillar: evidence *(planned)*

This pillar will collect research on

- the **psychological effects of AI** on people – for example trust, dependence, wellbeing, self-image and decisions – and
- the **use of AI in psychological work** – for example accuracy, effects on the working alliance and on practitioners' judgement.

Evidence is assessed separately from bindingness: a recommendation can be well supported by research without being binding, and a binding rule can lack evidence. Certainty will be rated following the logic of **GRADE** (high, moderate, low, very low).

Before this pillar is filled, its method will be defined in the [methodology](../docs/methodology.md#7-evidence-planned): search strategy, inclusion criteria, rating and how evidence is linked to the requirements in the [navigator](../navigator/).
