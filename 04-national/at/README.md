# Austria (AT)

Austrian law and professional rules relevant to AI in psychology and psychotherapy.

> **Status:** to be researched. Always check the current consolidated version in the [RIS](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at) (Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes).

## Core legal acts (L1)

| Act | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **Psychologengesetz 2013** | Professional duties of clinical and health psychologists (e.g. confidentiality, documentation, due diligence) |
| **Psychotherapiegesetz 2024** | Professional duties of psychotherapists |
| **Datenschutzgesetz (DSG)** | National supplement to the GDPR |
| **Medizinproduktegesetz 2021 (MPG 2021)** | National implementation of the MDR |
| **Gesundheitstelematikgesetz 2012 (GTelG 2012)** | Electronic health data, ELGA |
