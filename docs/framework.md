# Reference framework

ANIMA collects requirements that apply when AI enters psychological work or affects people psychologically. Some documents contain no such requirement, but set out the concepts and principles on which ANIMA builds – for example what health and mental health mean, the right to health, or a rights-based approach to mental health care. These documents form the **reference framework**.

## Role

- **Interpretation:** the reference framework is used to interpret terms and requirements in the sources – for example *mental health* in the broad sense used by the WHO (see the [scope](scope.md)).
- **No requirements:** documents of the reference framework do not appear as requirements in the [navigator](../navigator/README.md). Where a foundational document contains at least one requirement that applies to AI systems, it is a source instead.
- **Selection:** documents are selected in the same search and screening as all sources and recorded in the [candidate register](../search/README.md) with the decision `framework` at the full-text stage. The rule is set out in the [search protocol](search-protocol.md#reference-framework).
