## About ANIMA

### What is ANIMA?

An open European framework that shows what applies, what is recommended and what is known when AI enters psychological work or affects people psychologically. All sources – law, guidance of public bodies and standards of professional bodies – are placed on one model: the [triad](../model/README.md) of client, practitioner and AI system.

### Is ANIMA legal advice?

No. ANIMA provides orientation. It does not replace the original sources, legal advice or professional judgement. Always check the original sources and, where needed, consult qualified professionals.

### Is ANIMA an official document of the WHO, the EU or a professional body?

No. ANIMA is an independent initiative. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of any of the bodies whose sources it describes.

### Why is everything marked as "draft"?

ANIMA is in its build-up phase. Content counts as `reviewed` only after a second person has checked it (see the [methodology](methodology.md#5-verification)). Until reviewers are on board, all content stays `draft`.

### Why are psychotherapy and psychiatry not covered?

ANIMA focuses on psychology as a profession and on psychological effects of AI. Psychotherapy and psychiatry are not covered as professions – their own laws and professional bodies are out of scope. Their settings are covered where psychological effects of AI are concerned, for example when a person in psychotherapy uses a chatbot between sessions. See the [scope](scope.md#boundary-to-psychotherapy-and-psychiatry).

### How are sources selected?

Following a documented [search protocol](search-protocol.md) based on established practice for scoping reviews. Every candidate and every decision – including the reason for each exclusion – is recorded in the public [candidate register](../search/candidates.csv).

### How can I contribute?

By proposing sources, reporting errors, or writing source files. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

### How do I cite ANIMA?

Haas, J. G. (2026). *ANIMA – AI Navigator for Informed Mental Health Applications* [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24138892 – see also [How to cite](../README.md#how-to-cite).
