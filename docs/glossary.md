# Glossary

The terms used in ANIMA, in alphabetical order. Each entry gives a short definition and points to the document where the term is defined in full. Codes in `code style` are the values used in source files (see the [vocabulary](vocabulary.md)).

> **Status:** `draft` – first version, built from the terms already defined in ANIMA. Terms from the sources (e.g. *provider* and *deployer* in the AI Act) are added as the sources are included. German equivalents are listed at the end. Progress: [#19](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/19).

**Addressee** – the party that must act on a requirement: `practitioner`, `provider`, `institution`, `authority` or `client`. → [Model, section 6](../model/README.md#6-addressee)

**AI system** – a machine-based system as defined in Art. 3(1) of the EU AI Act; one of the three nodes of the triad (`AI`). The node stands for the system, not for the company behind it. → [Model, section 1](../model/README.md#1-nodes)

**Bindingness** – how binding a source is: `binding` (law), `official` (interpretation by an authority), `recommended` (recommendation by a public or international body) or `professional` (standard of a professional body). → [Methodology, section 2](methodology.md#2-pillars-and-bindingness)

**Candidate** – a document found in the search that may become a source. Every candidate is recorded in the candidate register and screened. → [Search protocol](search-protocol.md)

**Client** – the person with or about whom psychological work is done, or who is psychologically affected by an AI system – for example a client in counselling, a test taker, an applicant, or a person who uses a chatbot on their own; one of the three nodes of the triad (`C`). → [Model, section 1](../model/README.md#1-nodes)

**Configuration** – a typical situation of the triad, defined by which edges are active, missing or hidden; numbered `1` to `6`, from the full triad to AI use without direct client contact. → [Model, section 4](../model/README.md#4-configurations)

**Draft / reviewed** – the status of content: `draft` until a second person has checked it, `reviewed` afterwards. → [Methodology, section 5](methodology.md#5-verification)

**Edge** – a relationship within the triad: `C-P` (client – practitioner), `C-AI` (client – AI system), `P-AI` (practitioner – AI system) or `F-AI` (frame – AI system). → [Model, section 2](../model/README.md#2-edges)

**Evidence** – the planned fourth pillar: research on psychological effects of AI and on AI use in psychological work, rated following GRADE. → [evidence/](../evidence/README.md)

**Exclusion reason** – the reason why a candidate is not included, coded `E1` to `E8`. → [Search protocol, section 3](search-protocol.md#exclusion-reasons)

**Flow** – what a requirement regulates along an edge: `information`, `influence`, `relationship` or `responsibility`. → [Model, section 3](../model/README.md#3-flows)

**Frame** – everything around the triad that shapes it: provider, law, institution and environment. → [Model, section 5](../model/README.md#5-frame)

**Gap** – a combination of edge, flow and configuration for which no source assigns any duty. Gaps are shown in the navigator and never filled with assumptions. → [Methodology, section 3](methodology.md#3-mapping-onto-the-triad)

**GRADE** – an established system for rating the certainty of evidence (high, moderate, low, very low); planned for the evidence pillar. → [Methodology, section 7](methodology.md#7-evidence-planned)

**Grey literature** – documents published outside academic journals, such as laws, guidelines, codes and reports. Most sources of ANIMA are grey literature. → [Search protocol, section 2](search-protocol.md#2-methodological-basis)

**Guidance** – the pillar of recommendations by international and public bodies and of official interpretations of the law. → [sources/guidance/](../sources/guidance/README.md)

**International reference** – a source from outside the EU (e.g. from the United Kingdom or the United States), included as good practice; not binding in the EU. → [Scope, section 2](scope.md#2-sources)

**Jurisdiction** – where a source applies or who issued it: `global`, `europe`, `eu` or a country code such as `at`. → [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md#jurisdiction)

**Law** – the pillar of binding law. → [sources/law/](../sources/law/README.md)

**Mental health** – in the broad sense of the WHO: a state of mental well-being that enables people to cope with stress, realise their abilities and take part in their community; more than the absence of mental disorders. → [Scope, section 4](scope.md#4-definitions)

**Navigator** – the collection of all requirements, placed on the triad, with coverage and gaps; generated from the source files. → [navigator/](../navigator/README.md)

**Pillar** – a group of sources of the same kind: `law`, `guidance`, `professional`, and later `evidence`. → [Scope, section 2](scope.md#2-sources)

**Practitioner** – a psychologist who carries professional responsibility for the psychological work; one of the three nodes of the triad (`P`). → [Model, section 1](../model/README.md#1-nodes)

**Professional** – the pillar of codes, guidelines and statements of professional bodies of psychologists. → [sources/professional/](../sources/professional/README.md)

**Psychological effects of AI** – effects of AI systems on people's experience and behaviour, whether intended or not, and whether or not a practitioner is involved. → [Scope, section 4](scope.md#4-definitions)

**Requirement** – a single obligation, prohibition or recommendation taken from a source; the unit that is mapped onto the triad. → [Methodology, section 3](methodology.md#3-mapping-onto-the-triad)

**Scoping review** – a form of evidence synthesis that maps what exists on a topic; ANIMA's search follows its methods. → [Search protocol, section 2](search-protocol.md#2-methodological-basis)

**Source** – a document included in ANIMA after screening, stored as one file in `sources/`. → [sources/](../sources/README.md)

**Spectrum** – where an application lies between `wellbeing`, `prevention` and `diagnostics-treatment`. → [Scope, section 3](scope.md#3-spectrum)

**Topic** – a thematic group of requirements, such as `TRANS` (transparency) or `CRIS` (crisis and emergency handling). → [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md#topic)

**Triad** – the model of ANIMA: client, practitioner and AI system, with their edges, flows, configurations, frame and addressees. → [Model](../model/README.md)
