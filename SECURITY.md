# Security and sensitive reports

ANIMA is a content framework, not software. Security here mainly means the integrity of the content and the protection of personal data.

## What to report privately

Please **do not** open a public issue for:

- personal or health data of identifiable people that was published in the repository by mistake;
- content that could cause harm if left online (e.g. dangerous advice in the context of a crisis);
- signs that the repository, its workflows or its releases have been tampered with.

Instead, use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting** (tab *Security* → *Report a vulnerability*) or contact a maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) via their GitHub profile.

## What happens next

The maintainers confirm receipt as soon as possible, remove personal data without delay and, where necessary, also from the Git history and any affected Zenodo version.

## Errors in content

Factual or legal errors that are not sensitive can be reported publicly with the [error report form](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/new?template=error-report.yml).
