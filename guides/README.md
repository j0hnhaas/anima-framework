# Practice guides

> **Status:** concept. Guides are written only after the sources relevant to their situation have been included, so that every statement can be traced to a source. Progress: [#21](https://github.com/j0hnhaas/anima-framework/issues/21).

Practice guides answer concrete questions from practice, such as *"What do I need to consider when I use AI in psychological assessment?"*. Each guide starts from a situation, places it on the [triad](../model/README.md) and brings together what applies, what is recommended and what is known – drawn from the [navigator](../navigator/README.md).

## Structure of a guide

Every guide follows the same structure:

1. **Situation** – who does what with which AI system, in plain words.
2. **Place on the triad** – configuration, active edges, flows concerned.
3. **What applies** – binding requirements, with their source and addressee.
4. **What is recommended** – guidance and professional standards.
5. **What is known** – evidence, once the evidence pillar exists.
6. **Gaps and open questions** – where no source gives an answer.
7. **Checklist** – the points to check, each linked to its requirement in the navigator.

A guide never adds requirements of its own. Everything it says comes from the navigator; where the sources are silent, the guide says so.
